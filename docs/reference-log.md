# Design reference log

| Reference | Pattern studied | Principle used | What changed for Morrow |
|---|---|---|---|
| [LearningFirst](https://learningfirst.io/) | Structured collections, AI-supported learning, visible progress | Explain the product as a continuing learning journey, not isolated chat answers | Morrow starts from learner-selected topics and uses a distinct, conversation-led workflow |
| [Baymard online-learning benchmark](https://baymard.com/ux-benchmark/collections/online-learning) | Learning-site information architecture and decision support | Make product purpose and next actions easy to understand | A direct homepage story, clear learning modes, FAQ, and task-oriented workspace navigation |
| [UI Discovery: Edtech websites](https://uidiscovery.com/websites/edtech) | Marketing page section patterns | A product homepage needs more than a hero: capabilities, outcomes, objection handling, and a final action | Custom section order and original copy based on Morrow's learning model |
| [OpenDesign editorial product study](https://open-design.ai/showcase/editorial-product-website-design/) | Typographic hierarchy and paced product narrative | Treat the homepage as a designed explanation rather than a dashboard preview | Kept the editorial pacing, rejected the warm-paper/serif/terracotta visual recipe flagged by the Playbook |

References are research inputs, not assets or templates. No layouts, brand marks, illustrations, or copy are reproduced.
